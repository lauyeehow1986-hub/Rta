The Singapore COE Utilization Paradox Explained:

On a per-capita basis, Singapore ranks among the safest nations on earth with only 2.44 deaths per 100,000 population (close to Sweden's 2.00, Japan's 2.10, and the UK's 2.50). However, because Singapore restricts car ownership via the Vehicle Quota System (only 165.7 vehicles per 1,000 population versus 600–740 in OECD nations), each vehicle logs far higher daily exposure, and roads are shared with a high density of two-wheelers. Consequently, on a per-vehicle basis, Singapore suffers 11.0 fatalities per 100,000 registered vehicles — 2.6× to 3.2× higher than Japan (3.4) and Sweden (3.8).
